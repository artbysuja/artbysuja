The Artist

A Passion Begun Late,
Pursued with Devotion

Sujatha picked up a brush for the first time just over a year ago and hasn't stopped since. Working in acrylics from her home in Hyderabad, she has already completed over a hundred original works — landscapes, florals, abstracts, and beyond. Each painting is a little experiment in colour and feeling, made purely for the love of it.
